## GREEN — Permitted without prior approval

AI tools may be used for spelling, grammar, and clarity suggestions on text I have already drafted; brainstorming and outlining before I write; code explanation, debugging, and boilerplate scaffolding; literature discovery where every candidate paper is then read and verified in the original source; and formatting or structuring content I have already produced. Every output from a GREEN use is a starting point, not a final product, and must be reviewed and revised before use.

---

## AMBER — Permitted only with supervisor approval, full disclosure, and a prompt log

Paragraph-level drafting of non-core sections based on material I have read, data cleaning scripts, language polishing, and summaries of papers I have read may be useful with careful author review. For each AMBER use I must: obtain prior written approval from Dr. Loveleen Gaur, disclose the use in the relevant document, and record the date, tool, prompt summary, and my review decision in the prompt log at `reflections/prompt_log.md`.

**AMBER is not a loophole. If I cannot produce the log entry, the use was RED.**

---

## RED — Never, even if nobody would know

AI must not design the GNN architecture, select hyperparameters, or make methodological decisions. It must not write the discussion, conclusions, or abstract of the thesis or any derived publication. It must not generate reflective writing on my behalf, write peer reviews I sign, fabricate data, citations, or experimental results, or alter reported numbers. It must not process or describe non-public UIF operational data under any circumstances. Any use I would not disclose openly in this policy is RED by definition.

## Operating rules

1. Every citation must be checked against the original source before use. A tool-suggested reference is a search lead, not a citation.
2. Every code cell committed to the repository must have been executed and inspected by me personally.
3. I do not backdate commits or replace earlier versions without a Git commit that records the change.
4. When a use falls near the boundary between GREEN and AMBER, I treat it as AMBER, seek approval, and log it.
5. Before any thesis submission or public release, I re-read this policy and update the disclosure statement if my actual practice has changed.
